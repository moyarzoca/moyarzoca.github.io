**Semisimple Lie Algebras and Dynkin Classification**

- Classification: Aₗ, Bₗ, Cₗ, Dₗ, E₆, E₇, E₈, F₄, G₂
- Real sections (maximally split vs compact)
- Coset manifolds G/H and the Iwasawa decomposition
- Example: SL(3,ℂ) and coset SL(3,ℝ)/SO(3)
